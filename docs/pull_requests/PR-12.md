# Pull Request 12: Receipt Business Rules Validation

## Title
`feat(finance): implement comprehensive receipt business rules validation service`

## Overview
This PR enforces rigorous business rules on receipt creation and modifications. It introduces `receiptValidationService` to prevent financial corruption, enforce strict quantity and pricing boundaries, merge duplicate line items according to POS standards, validate discount thresholds, enforce tax rules, and verify cash payment sufficiency.

## Key Changes
1. **`ReceiptValidationService` (`backend/src/services/receiptValidationService.js`)**:
   - **Line-Item Validation & Normalization**:
     - Ensures receipts contain at least 1 and at most 100 items.
     - Enforces integer quantities strictly between 1 and 1,000.
     - Enforces unit prices between 0 and 1,000,000.
     - Automatically merges duplicate items for the same flavour and unit price (e.g. 2 Papaya + 3 Papaya => 5 Papaya).
   - **Discount Rule Enforcement**:
     - Prevents negative discounts.
     - Enforces that percentage discounts do not exceed 100%.
     - Enforces that fixed discounts cannot exceed the order subtotal.
   - **Tax Rule Enforcement**:
     - Rejects tax rates exceeding 100% or below 0% when tax is enabled.
   - **Financial Upper Bounds**:
     - Restricts order subtotal and total amount to 50,000,000 to prevent arithmetic and storage overflows.
   - **Payment Verification**:
     - Rejects unsupported payment methods.
     - Rejects cash transactions when received cash is insufficient for the total amount.

2. **Integration with `ReceiptService` (`backend/src/services/receiptService.js`)**:
   - Integrates `receiptValidationService` into `create` and `update` pipelines.
   - Re-runs business rule validation when receipts are edited.

3. **Payload Schema Updates (`backend/src/validators/receiptValidators.js`)**:
   - Added validation support for `cashReceived` and payment verification attributes.
